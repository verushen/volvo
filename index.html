What comes next

The Road Ahead studio can become Volvo's standing creator base for the next model moments

The studio showed that creators will come in, make strong work and carry it to their own audiences. These are the ways to build on it.

01

Give the best reels a longer life

Carl Thompson, Aaron Francis, Izzy Hammond, Cy Robinson, Josh Cuthbert and Aleks all beat their usual views. Their reels are ready made assets for UK buyers in the order window.

02

Send the cars home for a second wave

A week with the car lets creators show the electric school run, what 200 km of range means day to day, and the new Google built in software. Carl Thompson's week with the XC60 shows the format.

03

Open the studio to YouTube

YouTube carried two thirds of launch engagement. Invite the reviewers who covered the launch, like Autogefühl, What Car? and carwow, into the set for long form deep dives.

04

Cut TikTok first edits

Izzy Hammond and Jonas' Autovlog showed what native TikTok content can do. Shoot vertical cuts made for the platform in the same sessions.

05

Give the EX40 its own story

EX40 conversation doubled while it shared the stage with the XC40. Electric only content on range and charging would build on that momentum.

06

Take the format to more markets

Poland, Germany, Spain and Australia are already talking about these cars, and the long range XC60 and XC90 are confirmed for Australia. The studio model travels.

07

Book the next sessions around real moments

UK EX60 deliveries, the XC90 long range plug in hybrid and the March 2027 UK plate change are natural next dates for the set.

08

Run one creator calendar for Volvo UK

The Road Ahead, She's Electric and the ambassador programme reached many of the same audiences. One shared plan lets each build on the others.
